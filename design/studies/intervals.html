Intervals

Six ways to draw the 95% interval for two or three models, all on the same data. The goal: no clutter, and every band's edges readable exactly, even where bands overlap. Hover or tap any chart for exact values. Click a model's label to focus it.

Judged against intervals-goals.md. US, weekly flu hospital admissions.

What to check

  • With three models, which is the most uncertain three weeks out? How long did that take?
  • Where two bands overlap, can you still trace both edges of each?
  • Do the medians and observed data still read first?
  • Switch to Apart: is disagreement obvious? Switch to 8: does it fall apart gracefully?
  • Step back to an earlier date with Misses on: are the marked weeks easy to find without shouting?
  • Try Phone and the light theme.
